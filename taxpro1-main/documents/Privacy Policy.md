# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

TaxProPath ("TaxProPath", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use taxpropath.com and portal.taxpropath.com (together, the "Site"), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By using the Site, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

---

## 1. Who we are

TaxProPath is based at 3257 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Suite 1135, Washington, DC 20006, United States. We sell self-study continuing education courses for tax professionals. For any privacy question, email **support@taxpropath.com** or call +1 (458) 232-1511.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account and billing details:** name, email address, phone number, and billing address (street, city, state, ZIP code and country). If you choose, your company name.
- **Professional details:** your Preparer Tax Identification Number (PTIN), which we need to report your continuing education credit to the IRS.
- **Messages:** what you send us by email, through the contact form, through the chat on our Site, or when you sign up for our newsletter.

**Information created when you buy and study**

- **Orders:** the courses and add-ons you buy, prices, coupon codes, and the PayPal transaction ID. Payments are handled by PayPal. **We never see or store your full card or bank details.**
- **Course activity:** how much of a course video you have watched, your answers to in-course questions, your completion status, and the certificates issued to you.
- **Sign-in:** on taxpropath.com, your password (stored only in a securely hashed form) and the one-time codes (OTP) we email you; on your portal, the one-time 6-digit codes we email you.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Technical data:** IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.
- **Cookies:** see section 6.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- create your account and let you sign in;
- process your payment, deliver your courses and add-ons, and handle refunds;
- track your course progress and issue your certificates;
- report your earned continuing education credit to the IRS using your PTIN;
- send you service emails, such as order confirmations, sign-in codes, certificates and important account notices;
- answer your questions and give you support;
- send you news and offers about our courses, if you have not opted out (see section 7);
- keep the Site secure, prevent fraud, and fix problems;
- understand how the Site is used so we can improve it;
- meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

## 4. Your PTIN

We use your PTIN **only** to report your continuing education credit to the IRS and to keep the records the IRS requires of continuing education providers. We **never** print your PTIN on a certificate, and we **never** sell it or use it for marketing.

Please make sure the PTIN you give us is correct. If it is wrong or missing, we cannot report your credit to the IRS.

## 5. Who we share your information with

We share your information only as described here:

- **The IRS:** your name, PTIN and course completion details, to report your continuing education credit.
- **PayPal:** to process your payment and any refund. PayPal's use of your information is covered by PayPal's own privacy policy.
- **Service providers:** companies that help us run the Site, such as website hosting, database, email delivery, customer support and analytics providers. They may use your information only to provide their services to us and must keep it confidential.
- **Legal reasons:** when the law requires it, for example in response to a court order or a valid request from a government authority, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.
- **Business transfer:** if TaxProPath or its business is sold, merged or transferred, your information may be transferred as part of that deal. The new owner must follow this policy.
- **With your permission:** in any other case, only if you agree.

**We do not sell or rent your personal information.**

## 6. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use:

- **Essential cookies:** to keep you signed in and remember your cart. The Site does not work properly without them.
- **Preference cookies:** to remember choices such as light or dark mode.
- **Analytics cookies:** to understand how visitors use the Site, so we can improve it.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, some parts of the Site, such as the cart and checkout, may not work.

## 7. Emails from us

- **Service emails** (order confirmations, sign-in codes, certificates, refund updates) are part of using the Site. We will always send them.
- **Marketing emails** (new courses, offers, newsletter) are optional. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email support@taxpropath.com with the subject line "Unsubscribe".

## 8. How long we keep your information

- **Account details:** while your account is open, and for a reasonable time after it is closed.
- **Course completion, certificate and PTIN reporting records:** for as long as IRS rules for continuing education providers require, and for a reasonable time after that so we can reissue certificates and answer questions.
- **Order and payment records:** for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
- **Marketing list:** until you unsubscribe.

When we no longer need your information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## 9. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), limited staff access, passwords stored only in a securely hashed form, and sign-in by one-time email codes. No method of storing or sending information over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password and your email account secure, because sign-in codes are sent to your email.

## 10. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct information that is wrong;
- delete your information (we may need to keep some records, such as completion and payment records, where the law requires it);
- stop sending you marketing emails.

Email **support@taxpropath.com** with your request. We may need to confirm your identity first. We reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

## 11. Children

The Site is for professionals and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Where your information is stored

Our courses are intended for tax professionals in the United States. Your information may be stored and processed in the United States or in other countries where our service providers operate.

## 13. Links to other websites

The Site may link to other websites, such as PayPal or the IRS. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their privacy policies.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you by email or with a notice on the Site. Using the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
