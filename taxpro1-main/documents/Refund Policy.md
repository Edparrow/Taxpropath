# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

TaxProPath ("TaxProPath", "we", "us") sells self-study continuing education courses for tax professionals at taxpropath.com. This policy explains when you can get your money back and how to ask.

Our promise is simple: if something goes wrong on our side, we will fix it or refund you.

---

## 1. When you can get a full refund

You can get a full refund of the course price if **any** of these apply:

- **Changed your mind:** you ask within **7 days** of purchase, **and** you have not started the course (you have not played the course video), **and** no certificate has been issued for it.
- **Duplicate purchase:** you were charged twice for the same course, or you bought a course you already own.
- **Our technical fault:** a problem on our side stops you from accessing or completing the course, and we cannot fix it within **5 business days** of you reporting it.
- **Course withdrawn:** we withdraw the course before you have completed it.

## 2. When we cannot give a refund

We are not able to refund a course when:

- you have started the course (played the course video), unless the problem was caused by us;
- a certificate (IRS CE or attendance) has already been issued for the course;
- more than 7 days have passed since purchase (except for duplicate purchases, our technical faults or withdrawn courses);
- the problem is on your side, such as your device, browser, internet connection, firewall or email filters (we are always happy to help you troubleshoot);
- you did not meet the requirements for a certificate, for example because you did not finish the video, did not answer the in-course questions, or did not give us a valid PTIN. Please contact us and we will help you complete the requirements.

## 3. Add-ons (e-Transcript and Slides)

e-Transcripts and slides are digital downloads.

- If you refund a course before you start it, any add-ons bought with it are refunded too, as long as they have not been downloaded.
- You can cancel just an add-on and keep the course. If the add-on has not been downloaded, we refund that add-on's price.
- Once an add-on has been downloaded, it cannot be refunded.

## 4. Swap instead of a refund

Bought the wrong course? If you have not started it, you can swap it for another course instead of taking a refund. Ask within 7 days of purchase. If the new course costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference.

## 5. How to ask for a refund

Email **support@taxpropath.com** with the subject line **"Refund request"** and include:

- your order number;
- the email address you used to buy;
- the course name and the reason for your request.

We reply within **2 business days**. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method (PayPal) within **5 business days** of approval. PayPal or your bank may take a few more days to show it.

When a course is refunded, your access to it ends and no certificate will be issued for it.

## 6. Payment disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute or chargeback with PayPal or your bank. Most problems are solved quickly by email. While a dispute is open, we may pause access to the course involved.

## 7. Not happy with our answer?

If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to our email and ask for a review. A senior member of our team will look at it again and reply within **10 business days**.
